Send USDC from one chain to another with no on-chain link between deposit and payout. The Erebuz protocol handles bridging through Circle CCTP, privacy through Railgun, and orchestration through a TEE.

[wall8](https://app.erebuz.com) is the reference app built on the protocol.

## How a transfer works

The protocol moves USDC through three legs:

1. **Bridge to the hub.** USDC is burnt on the source chain via Circle CCTP and minted on Arbitrum (the privacy hub). The sender sends USDC to a TEE-managed smart account on the source chain.
2. **Shield and unshield.** USDC is shielded into a Railgun pool then unshielded to a fresh address. This breaks the on-chain link between the two legs.
3. **Bridge to the destination.** USDC is burnt on Arbitrum and minted to the recipient on the destination chain.

The TEE handles everything after the deposit.

## Privacy model

An observer sees USDC burnt on the source chain and USDC minted from the Railgun pool on the destination. The shielded pool sits between them so the burn cannot be tied to the mint, or the sender to the recipient.

The TEE knows the full route. Privacy is against outside observers, not the operator.

## Fee breakdown

The total fee bundles three components, charged on the USDC output:

- Service fee: `max($1, 1.5%)`
- Railgun unshield fee: 0.25%
- CCTP bridge fee: ~0.03%

The quote endpoint returns each component separately (`feeAmount`, `privacyFeeAmount`, `bridgeFeeAmount`).

## Supported chains

CCTP-supported chains only. USDC is the only bridged token. No slippage, 1:1 peg.

Testnet: Sepolia, Base Sepolia, Arbitrum Sepolia, OP Sepolia, Polygon Amoy, Unichain Sepolia, World Chain Sepolia, Sei Atlantic-2, Arc Testnet

Mainnet (expanding): Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum

List available chains and tokens:

```bash
curl $BASE_URL/api/relay/chains
curl "$BASE_URL/api/relay/tokens?chainId=42161&search=USDC"
```
